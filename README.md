# Lunette

Support pages and privacy policy for **Lunette** (on the App Store as *Lunette: Moon Phase & Sun*), a macOS
menu bar app that shows tonight's Moon and the day's Sun times.

The site is served by GitHub Pages at <https://azun-k2.github.io/lunette/>:

| Page | URL |
|---|---|
| Home | <https://azun-k2.github.io/lunette/> |
| Support | <https://azun-k2.github.io/lunette/support.html> |
| Privacy policy | <https://azun-k2.github.io/lunette/privacy.html> |

The last two are the support and privacy policy URLs given to App Store Connect, so their paths should stay
put. Icons are regenerated from the app with `../_tools/make-site-icons.py` run in `../Lunette`.
